## What is Descriptive Analytics?
Descriptive Analytics is looking at your historical business data to understand _what happened ?_ Of course, what happened depends on your specific business and your specific use case. You might be interested in knowing how people shopped in your online store or how people serviced their debt for loans issued from your bank. This kind of analysis forms the basis or foundation for other types of analysis like _predictive_, or, _prescriptive_ analytics.
## What kind of use cases does this repository cover?
This repository covers operational or analytical datasets that are relational. In particular, _Large Language Models_ are not part of any solution here. Solutions developed here utilize machine learning methods that have served the _knowledge discovery in databases_ for a long time.
